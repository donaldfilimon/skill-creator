# Claim-honest law

Use with `07-family-and-bots.md`. Source of truth for Current vs Proposed is the Abbey/ABI ledgers, not marketing copy.

## Labels

- **Current** — proved in-repo by test, gate, or dated evidence file.
- **Partial** — implemented in part; do not upgrade in prose.
- **Proposed** — approved roadmap, not shipped.
- **Blocked** — external blocker (billing lock, missing host, missing consent).
- **Out of scope** — will not be claimed.

A target is never a result. MLAI provenance tags: measured ● / target ○ / reported ◆. If a number is not in `MLAI-master-reference.md`, do not ship it on the site.

## Refuse as Current

Do not invent or promote:

- Bake-off tables, empathy scores, QPS, latency, accuracy, energy-efficiency
- H100 / Kubernetes / multi-region shard theater
- Browser autonomy as shipped ABI behavior
- AES/RBAC or regulatory certifications without a cited artifact
- Merging Rust `abbey-bot` and Swift `AbbeyBot` into one runtime
- Replacing WDBX with Qdrant or pgvector
- Turning GitHub billing-lock red X into a product failure *or* a green
- Live Discord / consented voice as Current while evidence files say PENDING

## Apple sentence

Only: *Built on Apple's public frameworks — Metal, Accelerate, Core ML.*
No partnership, endorsement, sponsorship, or employer claim.

## Runtime honesty

- ABI reports `accelerated=false` when native kernels are not linked.
- Failed or skipped WDBX writes are reported, not fabricated as success.
- `abi complete --neural` is an in-process char-LM demo, not a production LLM.
- Cluster RPC is single-host / reference-scoped unless a ledger row says otherwise.

## Adult vs product

Product Aviva (expert mode) and roster/Grok Aviva (sex-fight) share a name and must not share claims. Adult scenes stay in adult skills. Site copy stays claim-honest.
